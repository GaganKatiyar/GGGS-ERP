# feature-separation

Separation cases, clearance, settlement, letters and alumni, and my resignation.

- `separation-pages.ts`: the dashboard and cases, a case (terms, exit reason, early release,
  clearance, exit interview, settlement, timeline), my clearance and my resignation.
- `separation-policy-page.ts`: notice periods and rules, leave during notice, settlement deadlines.
- `offboarding-pages.ts`: the offboarding panel on a case (handover, successor, sign-in, checklist)
  and on my resignation, the offboarding tasks queue and the checklist templates.
- `involuntary-pages.ts`: disciplinary (show-cause, enquiry and decision), absconding (alerts,
  notices, separation) and batch retrenchment.
- `insight-pages.ts`: attrition analytics and the exit survey editor.
